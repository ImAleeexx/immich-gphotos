Google Photos credential wizard

Extracts the auth_data string that immich-gphotos needs, straight from your phone over USB. Everything happens in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Takes about ten minutes.

1Prepare the phone

On the phone: Settings → About phone → tap "Build number" seven times, then Settings → System → Developer options → enable "USB debugging". Plug it into this computer with a cable that supports data.

Close Android Studio, VS Code and scrcpy first. They run an adb server that takes exclusive ownership of the phone, and this page will not be able to reach it.
2Connect

Pick your phone in the browser prompt, then watch the phone screen.

On the phone: a dialog says "Allow USB debugging?" — tick "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow. This page uses its own key, so it asks even if you've authorised this computer before.
not connected
3Download the two apps

Google offers no API for this, so Photos has to run against a stand-in for Play Services. Download both files to this computer — you'll send them to the phone in the next steps.

4Install GmsCore

Select the app.revanced.android.gms-….apk you downloaded.

Play Protect will warn you — this is expected. GmsCore deliberately impersonates Google Play Services, which is exactly the pattern Play Protect flags. On the phone you may see "Blocked by Play Protect" or "Unsafe app blocked":
  1. Tap More details (small, under the message)
  2. Tap Install anyway
If there's no "install anyway" option, tick the box below before installing.
5Install Google Photos

Select the googlephotos-devanced-….apk. It's the larger of the two.

This does not touch your existing Google Photos. The patched build installs under its own name (app.morphe.android.apps.photos), so both sit side by side and your normal app is untouched. You'll see a second Photos icon on the phone — that's the one the wizard opens.
6Sign in — the credential appears here

This page starts listening first, then opens the apps for you. Leave this tab open.

On the phone, in order:
  1. GmsCore opens. Tap through its checklist and allow what it asks for (battery optimisation, notifications).
  2. Open Account → Add Google account → sign in. This is the sign-in that produces the credential — not the one in Photos.
  3. Come back and press Open Google Photos, then choose that same account.
idle
7Your credential

Paste this into the immich-gphotos wizard. Treat it like a password — it can't be rotated easily, so don't paste it into a GitHub issue.

ready.
Nothing leaves this tab — what it talks to

api.github.com to look up download links, and cdn.jsdelivr.net to load the USB library. The APKs go from your disk to your phone over the USB cable. The credential is never sent anywhere.
